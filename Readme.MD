
🎯 InterviewPro AI – Intelligent Mock Interview & Feedback Agent

InterviewPro AI is an AI-powered mock interview application that helps students and job seekers practice interviews in an interactive and personalized way. The application uses Google Gemini AI to conduct interviews, evaluate candidate responses, provide constructive feedback, and generate a final performance report.

The project is built using Python and Streamlit and includes a Text-to-Speech feature that allows users to listen to AI-generated interview questions.

🚀 Features
🎤 AI-Powered Mock Interviews

Conduct an interactive mock interview with an AI interviewer.

💼 Multiple Interview Types

Users can choose from:

HR Interview
Technical Interview
Coding Interview
Aptitude Interview
Communication Interview
👨‍💻 Personalized Interview Setup

Configure the interview based on:

Job Role
Experience Level
Difficulty Level
Candidate Skills
Number of Questions
🤖 AI-Generated Questions

Google Gemini dynamically generates relevant interview questions based on the selected job role and interview configuration.

🔊 Text-to-Speech

Users can listen to AI-generated interview questions using the built-in Text-to-Speech feature.

📝 Answer Evaluation

The AI evaluates candidate answers and provides:

Score out of 10
Strengths
Weaknesses
Constructive Feedback
Improvement Suggestions
🧠 Adaptive Interview Questions

The next question is generated based on the candidate's previous question and answer, making the interview more personalized and interactive.

📊 Final Performance Report

After completing the interview, the AI generates a detailed report containing:

Overall Performance
Key Strengths
Areas for Improvement
Technical Skills Assessment
Communication Assessment
Interview Readiness
Personalized Improvement Plan
Final Recommendation
